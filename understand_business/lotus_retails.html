Lotus Retails is a fictional chain of supermarkets: stores in towns. Customers buy at the tills in the store. Follow one receipt on its way to a manager's pay. Then see who else reads the data, and what a wrong value does to them.

    When the data was wrong

      Every Monday the head of retail operations signs off the bonus. She asks: Can I pay the bonus from last week's net sales, store by store? And if I cannot trust a number, which one, and why? Today nobody can answer the second sentence. That is what this project teaches you to build.
