The alerts page

An alert tells the person who can act: what failed, how bad it is, and what to do. In a real team this page would be an email, a Slack channel, or a paging service such as PagerDuty. Here it stays on your computer, so no account is needed. Under Airflow these alerts are sent by a task callback. The decision about level and person is the same.
